Behind the Label

Built from the Ground Up in Pampanga

Founded by Nash Calma, stanburn.clo started with a simple goal craft accessible, well-proportioned streetwear staples that feel right at home on the streets of Angeles City.

From our signature boxy crop tees to everyday caps and vulcanized shoes, every piece is designed for comfort, durability, and effortless styling.

Execution & Supply Chain

Business Operations

Sourcing & Production

Working with reliable local manufacturers of 280 GSM cotton fabric, superior quality hardware, and assembling leather goods.

Operated on a small batch inventory system in order to avoid dead stock, high-quality control, and product exclusivity.

Direct pick-ups from Balibago, Angeles City, along with local couriers within Pampanga and throughout the Philippines.
